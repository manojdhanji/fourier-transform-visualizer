Fourier Transform Visualizer

Time Domain - Frequency Domain - Winding Diagram

Details

DFT Animation: Shows how each frequency component accumulates as a rotating phasor.

Color Legend:

  • Yellow – Trajectory (frequency present)
  • Cyan – Final vector (DFT coefficient)
  • Gray – Trajectory (frequency absent)
  • Red – Vanishing vector (absent frequency)
  • Dark gray – Trajectory (no DFT yet)
  • Light gray – Vector (no DFT yet)

Tip: Move the slider to explore how each frequency contributes to the signal.
